\cvsection{Cover Letter}

Dear Ms. Bernhard,

I am writing to apply for the CAD Designer R\&D position at Webasto in Grevenmacher. As a Mechanical Engineer with a strong interest in design, product development, and practical engineering solutions, I was genuinely excited when I read about this opportunity. The combination of innovation, sustainability, and user-focused mobility at Webasto is exactly the kind of environment I would be proud to be part of.

Over the past years, I have built a solid background in CAD and CAE through my studies, engineering projects, and professional experience. I have worked with several design and simulation tools, including CATIA, CREO, SolidWorks, Autodesk Inventor, Fusion~360, AutoCAD, and ANSYS. Because I have already learned and used different CAD platforms, I have become very comfortable applying the same engineering thinking across different tools and adapting efficiently to different project requirements. This experience has helped me build a flexible and practical approach to CAD work in product development environments.

What I enjoy most about design work is the full development cycle from concept to improvement. For me, design is not only about creating a model, but also about testing whether it performs well in simulation and later in real manufacturing conditions. After the first design is created, I believe it should be checked, analyzed, and improved based on simulation results, engineering evaluation, and manufacturing requirements. Going back to the design repeatedly to optimize it is an important part of developing a successful product. With a strong mechanical engineering background, I try to improve the design step by step until it becomes as efficient, practical, and reliable as possible, while also bringing it as close as possible to customer needs.

I also bring strong Microsoft Office skills, which I understand are important for this role. In Excel, I developed a cost estimation dashboard for evaporators and condensers based on the type and quantity of materials used. This helped make the estimation process more structured and efficient. I have also regularly prepared and delivered presentations in both university and company settings using PowerPoint, and I wrote and organized my bachelor project documentation in Word. These experiences helped me become confident in presenting technical information clearly and working in a structured way.

In addition, my internship at Ramin Thermal Power Plant gave me valuable hands-on exposure to industrial maintenance and overhaul processes, especially for shell-and-tube heat exchangers. That experience strengthened my practical engineering perspective and helped me better understand how design decisions connect to real industrial applications.

What attracts me most to Webasto is not only the technical side of the role, but also the company’s vision. I like the idea of contributing to products that improve mobility while also supporting a more sustainable future by using less material while maintaining the same level of safety and performance. I would be very happy to bring my motivation, adaptability, and engineering skills to your R\&D team.

Thank you very much for considering my application. I would be glad for the opportunity to discuss my background with you in more detail.
