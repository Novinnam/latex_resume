\cvsection{Cover Letter}

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am excited to apply for the Mechanical Designer - Molds (Product Development) position at Husky Technologies. What attracts me to this role is the chance to work on real product development challenges in a company known for innovation, quality, and strong engineering standards. I enjoy taking a technical problem, understanding it deeply, and turning it into a practical and reliable design.

Let me tell you a story. In my current role, we had a problem with cameras operating in a dusty industrial environment. Dust particles in the air were scratching the camera lenses, and the required lens size was very specific and not easily available on the market. To solve this, I worked on designing a custom camera housing with an integrated air knife to protect the lens.

Before starting the engineering work, I first prepared a technical specification to make sure my understanding of the problem was fully aligned with the company’s needs. After that, I moved into the engineering phase: I carried out the design work, performed the necessary calculations, studied the airflow behavior, optimized the geometry, checked the structural integrity, and then adapted the part for FFF 3D printing and manufacturing. What I really liked about this project was that it required both analytical thinking and practical engineering decisions.

This reflects the way I like to work. For simpler designs, I do the calculations directly using well-known mechanical engineering equations. For more complex geometries or loading conditions, I use simulation tools such as CREO for CAD and ANSYS for CAE, including FEA and CFD analysis, to better understand structural behavior and fluid performance. I enjoy combining theory, simulation, and practical design implementation.

Beyond this project, I have also worked on shell-and-tube heat exchanger overhaul activities, including assembly and disassembly at Ramin Power Plant, developed a single power screw press at the University of Luxembourg, and contributed to the design of HVAC components such as evaporators and condensers with attention to heat transfer and manufacturing requirements. These experiences helped me build a solid foundation in mechanical design, calculations, simulation, and production-aware engineering.

I am particularly interested in this opportunity at Husky because it combines product development, technical problem-solving, CAD design, and collaboration across teams. I believe my hands-on approach, my motivation to solve real engineering problems, and my experience with both analytical and simulation-based design would allow me to contribute positively to your team.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would be happy to discuss my background and motivation further.
